# Virtual assistant security checklist research

- Date checked: 2026-07-25 UTC
- Planned slug: `/blog/virtual-assistant-security-checklist`
- Site boundary: Filipino remote assistants only; no local or in-person candidate claim.

## Primary evidence checked before drafting

1. FBI, 2024 IC3 Annual Report: https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2024_IC3Report.pdf
   - HTTP 200, PDF.
   - The report says 2024 complaints totaled 859,532.
   - It says 256,256 complaints reported an actual loss.
   - It reports a 33 percent increase in losses from 2023.
   - It says IC3 averaged more than 2,000 complaints per day during the past five years.
   - Exact quote checked in the signed introduction: "We want to be there for you, and what you report will help us help others."
   - Attribution: B. Chad Yarbrough, Operations Director for Criminal and Cyber, Federal Bureau of Investigation.

2. CISA, Use Strong Passwords: https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/use-strong-passwords
   - HTTP 200, HTML.
   - Says passwords should be at least 16 characters, random, and unique.

3. CISA, Turn on Multifactor Authentication: https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/turn-mfa
   - HTTP 200, HTML.
   - Supports adding another sign-in check beyond the password.

4. NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final
   - HTTP 200, HTML.
   - Primary source for least-privilege access control.

5. Republic Act No. 10173: https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10173_2012.html
   - HTTP 200, HTML.
   - Primary Philippine legal text for personal information processing and protection.

A separate National Privacy Commission PDF probe hit a network error, so it was not used as evidence or cited.

## Editorial choices

The article explains what the FBI figures can and cannot show. It does not turn reported complaints into a claim about every online event, every Filipino assistant, or this service. The copy uses short sentences, concrete role examples, owner approval limits, and no invented results or people.
